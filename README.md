# Sales Insights – Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes sales performance data to identify key business trends, revenue patterns, market performance, and customer insights using SQL and Power BI.

The project demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics workflow, including data exploration and querying with SQL, data modeling, KPI development, and interactive dashboard creation in Power BI.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance
- Identify high-performing and underperforming markets
- Understand sales trends over time
- Compare performance across different markets and regions
- Track key business KPIs
- Generate actionable insights to support business decision-making

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** – Data visualization and interactive dashboard development
- **SQL** – Data extraction, transformation, and analysis
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – Measures and KPI calculations
- **Data Modeling** – Relationships between fact and dimension tables

## 📊 Key Analysis Areas

### Sales Performance
Analysis of sales amount and overall business performance.

### Market Analysis
Comparison of sales performance across different markets.

### Revenue Trends
Analysis of sales trends over time to identify growth patterns and fluctuations.

### KPI Analysis
Development of business KPIs to monitor performance and support decision-making.
